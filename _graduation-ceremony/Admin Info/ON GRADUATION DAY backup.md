<h2><strong>GRADUANDS’ INFORMATION</strong></h2>
<p></p>
<h3><strong>REGISTRATION</strong></h3>
<p>Things to bring along for the Graduation Ceremony:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Graduation gown and hood</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Identification card or any other form of photographed identification</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Registration confirmation QR code from Joyous Group Holdings portal in
your mobile phone
<br>(You are not required to print the QR code.)</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Upon arrival, graduands are to register at NEXUS (Blk D Level 7) between
12pm to 1.30 pm. Please be punctual as <strong>LATECOMERS WILL <u>NOT</u> BE ALLOWED ON STAGE</strong>.</p>
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<h3><strong>ORDER OF PROCEEDINGS</strong></h3>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Upon the cue from marshals, graduands are to quietly leave their seats
row by row to queue for stage presentation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Verify your name with the marshal in the queue.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>When your name is announced, walk briskly across the stage, receive the
scroll/folder and face the photographers to have your photograph taken.
Proceed to walk to the other side of the stage.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>There will be marshals to guide you back to your seat in the Hall.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3><strong>COLLECTION OF CERTIFICATES</strong></h3>
<p>To ensure that all certificates are correctly issued, the scroll received
by graduands on stage WILL NOT contain any document.</p>
<p>Graduands will collect their certificates after exiting from the stage,
at the back of the Hall. If you do not wish to keep the empty scroll, we
encourage you to return it at the certificate collection point to support
our sustainability efforts by reducing waste. Graduands are to return to
their seats after collecting the certificate and remain seated for the
rest of the ceremony.</p>
<p></p>
<h3><strong>PHOTOGRAPHY</strong></h3>
<p>Each graduand will have two photograph shots taken on stage</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>A full length view – portrait orientation</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A close-up frontal shot of the graduand receiving his/her scroll – half
body, landscape orientation</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Graduands may place their orders for copies of the photographs with the
official photographer, Joyous Group Holdings. The official photographer
will be setting up an on-site mobile studio to provide photography services
for graduands and families. Due to the large number of graduands at the
venue, prior arrangement with the photographer for the family portraits
is recommended.</p>
<p><strong><em>Graduands are under no obligation to purchase from the vendor. All purchases are voluntary.</em></strong>
</p>
