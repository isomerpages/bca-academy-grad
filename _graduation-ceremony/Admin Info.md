<h3><strong>BEFORE GRADUATION DAY</strong></h3>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Registration of Attendance</strong>
</p>
<p>Graduands will be receiving an email invitation to the Graduation Ceremony.</p>
<p>Graduands are to complete the Graduation Survey and register their attendance
for the Graduation Ceremony via the link, username and password given on
completion of the Graduation Survey.</p>
<p></p>
<p>If a graduand fails to respond by the stipulated deadline, it will be
assumed that he/she will not be attending the ceremony and no arrangement
will be made for his/her attendance at the Graduation Ceremony.</p>
<p>Graduands must settle any outstanding loan of BCAA property and fees with
the Academy before they can attend the Graduation Ceremony or collect their
certificates (where applicable).</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Collection of Gown</strong>
</p>
<p>Official Gown Vendor: Werkz Studio Pte Ltd.&nbsp;
<br>
<br>Gown Collection Period is as follows:&nbsp;</p>
<p><u>Date/ Time:</u>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p>30, 31 October, 1 November and 11 November 2025, 11:00am to 7:00pm (subject
to selection of preferred time slot)</p>
<p><u>Venue:</u>
</p>
<p>Werkz Studio
<br>(10 Ubi Crescent Ubi Techpark Lobby D #01-66 Singapore 408564)</p>
<p>Date/ Time: &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; 15 November
2025, 11:00am to 1:30pm</p>
<p>Venue:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
BCA Braddell Campus (Block E Level 1)</p>
<p></p>
<p>Collect your graduation gown during the preferred date and time you have
indicated in your online registration.&nbsp;</p>
<p>A rental fee of S$35.00 will be charged by vendor. Graduands are to make
payment directly to the vendor during registration.</p>
<p>An additional S$65.00 will be collected by the vendor as deposit for the
graduation gown and hood. This amount will be refunded upon prompt return
of graduation gown and hood (in good condition) to the vendor.</p>
<p>Payment can only be made by PayNow or credit card directly to the vendor.</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Admission Tickets</strong>
</p>
<p>Purchase of guest admission tickets will be charged at a fee of S$40.00
(including GST) per ticket, subject to availability and balloting.</p>
<p>At Registration of Attendance, graduands will register for the number
of guest tickets that they intend to purchase.</p>
<p>The number of guest admission tickets available for purchase will be determined
after collating all registrations from the graduating cohort and graduands
will be notified on the number of guest admission ticket(s) allocated to
them after the registration period closes. You will be required to make
payment then and seats are confirmed upon receipt of payment.</p>
<p>Attending graduands who are Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
will be offered one complimentary guest admission ticket, subject to confirmation
in the registration portal.</p>
<p>Payment must be made by PayNow or credit card directly to the vendor.</p>
<p>Guests without valid admission ticket will not be allowed entry to witness
the ceremony proceedings. Misplaced admission tickets WILL NOT be replaced
nor re-issued.</p>
<p>Due to the nature of the event, it is recommended for guests to be at
least 7 years of age.</p>
<p>Graduands who require accessible seating or for their guests are to indicate
the requirement in Werkz Studio portal.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3><strong>ON GRADUATION DAY</strong></h3>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong>

<p><strong>[GRADUANDS’ INFORMATION]</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>REGISTRATION</strong>
</p>
<p>Things to bring along for the Graduation Ceremony:</p>
<p>a) Graduation gown and hood</p>
<p>b) Identification card or any other form of photographed identification</p>
<p>c) Attendance confirmation QR code from Werkz Studio portal in your mobile
phone (You are not required to print the QR code.)</p>
<p>Upon arrival, graduands are to register at Block E Level 1 between 12pm
to 1.30 pm. Please be punctual as <strong>LATECOMERS WILL <u>NOT</u> BE ALLOWED ON STAGE</strong>.</p>
<p>Graduands are to be seated according to assigned Seat No. at NEXUS (Block
D Level 7) by 1.30pm.</p>

when attending the Graduation Ceremony. Graduands who are inappropriately

<p>&nbsp;Traditional costumes are permitted.
<br>*Note: slippers and sandals are not permitted on stage.</p>
<p>&nbsp;The gown vendor will be on-site to assist graduands with their gowns
and hoods on that day.</p>
<p><strong>ORDER OF PROCEEDINGS</strong>
</p>
<p>Upon the cue from marshals, graduands are to quietly leave their seats
row by row to queue for stage presentation.</p>
<p>Verify your name with the marshal in the queue.</p>
<p>When your name is announced, walk briskly across the stage, receive the
scroll/folder and face the photographers to have your photograph taken.</p>
<p>Proceed to walk to the other side of the stage. There will be marshals
to guide you back to your seat in the Hall.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>COLLECTION OF CERTIFICATES</strong>
</p>
<p>To ensure that all certificates are correctly issued, the scroll received
by graduands on stage WILL NOT contain any document.</p>
<p>Graduands will collect their certificates after exiting from the stage,
at the back of the Hall.</p>
<p>If you do not wish to keep the empty scroll, we encourage you to return
it at the certificate collection point to support our sustainability efforts
by reducing waste.</p>
<p>Graduands are to return to their seats after collecting the certificate
and remain seated for the rest of the ceremony.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>PHOTOGRAPHY</strong>
</p>
<p>Each graduand will have two photograph shots taken on stage</p>
<p>a) A full-length view – portrait orientation</p>
<p>b) A close-up frontal shot of the graduand receiving his/her scroll –
half body, landscape orientation</p>
<p>Graduands may place their orders for copies of the photographs with the
official photographer, Werkz Studio Pte Ltd.</p>
<p>Attending graduands who are Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
will be offered two complimentary 5R digital on-stage photos, subject to
confirmation in the registration portal.</p>
<p>The official photographer will be setting up an on-site mobile studio
to provide photography services for graduands and families.</p>
<p>Due to the large number of graduands at the venue, prior arrangement with
the photographer for the family portraits is recommended.</p>
<p>Graduands are under no obligation to purchase from the vendor.</p>
<p>All purchases are voluntary.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>[GUESTS’ INFORMATION]</strong>
</p>
<p>&nbsp;<strong>REGISTRATION</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong>All guests are to be accompanied by the respective
graduands, and to proceed to Block E Level 1.</p>
<p><strong>ADMISSION INTO NEXUS</strong>
</p>
<p>Admission to NEXUS (Block D Level 7) will be strictly controlled and permitted
only with valid guest admission tickets (in the form of QR code and you
are not required to print it out).</p>
<p>One admission ticket admits one guest only. Admission tickets are non-transferrable.</p>
<p>Guests are to be seated within the designated sections and marshals will
be on site for guidance.</p>
<p>Guests <strong><u>are not permitted</u></strong> to enter the seating rows/sections
for graduands.</p>
<p>Guests must remain seated throughout the Graduation Ceremony, and refrain
from standing up to avoid obstructing other audience and the proceedings.</p>
