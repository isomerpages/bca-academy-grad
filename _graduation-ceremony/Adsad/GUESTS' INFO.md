<p><strong>ADMISSION INTO THE HALL</strong>
</p>
<p>Admission to the Hall will be strictly controlled and permitted only with
valid guest admission tickets (in the form of QR code and you are not required
to print it out).</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>One admission ticket admits one guest only. Admission tickets are non-transferrable.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Guests are to be seated within the designated sections and marshals will
be on site for guidance.</p>
<p>Guests <strong><u>are not permitted</u></strong> to enter the seating rows/sections
for graduands.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Guests must remain seated throughout the Graduation Ceremony, and refrain
from standing up to avoid obstructing other audience and the proceedings.</p>
